## Problem

At 5 people, everyone knows where things live. At 25 people, knowledge is split across finance, communications, engineering, and whoever wrote the last report. The document exists. The problem is finding it — and interrupting the person who remembers.

That tax shows up as slow onboarding, repeated “where is the template?” messages, and senior staff acting as a human search engine.

Generic chat tools make it worse: they answer from the internet, not from *your* files, and they sound confident when they are wrong.

## Solution

Athena sits on top of the docs you already have (Google Docs, Notion, uploads, internal forms). A teammate asks a question. Athena returns an answer grounded in those sources, with citations.

What makes it usable in real organisations:

- **Your knowledge only.** Answers come from the org’s documents, not the public web.
- **Honest gaps.** Below the confidence threshold, it does not fabricate.
- **Your compute, if you need it.** Cloud for most teams; deploy on a customer’s own server when data cannot leave the building.
- **Minutes to go live.** Connect sources and start asking. No new wiki to build first.
